Brat Pixel Sans v5

Install all four TTF files. In Figma select Brat Pixel Sans v5, then choose Regular, Medium, Semi Bold or Bold.

Regular 400; Medium 500; Semi Bold 600; Bold 700.
The Regular outlines preserve v3. Medium and Semi Bold are outline-expanded derivatives of the regular base. Bold uses the matching Liberation Sans Bold base. Pixel alternates have separately increased stroke weight.
Latin and Cyrillic are included; Cyrillic uses the source forms, with custom pixel alternates for all Bulgarian letters. The smooth base retains the source Cyrillic forms.

calt Latin: d after e; e after l; x after E. Cyrillic: е after л; н after а; з after и; р after ш; с after к.
ss01: manually switch selected supported characters to pixel alternates.
All four weights contain full Bulgarian uppercase/lowercase pixel variants, including Й/й and Ѝ/ѝ, plus the Latin variants and private-use aliases.

Custom derivative of Liberation Sans under SIL OFL 1.1. This family is not Soehne; the Klim specimen was used as a reference for the requested 400/500/600/700 weight range.
