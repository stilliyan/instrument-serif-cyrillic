# Regular 0.560 — lowercase rhythm, shared oval and round tail caps

The user identified х as visually too high and inconsistent in both form and
spacing in “Вдъхновение”. They also asked for approximately matching ъ/ь ovals
and rounded endings on ц/щ descenders. The before/after proof uses actual 0.551
and 0.560 exports at the same em. Original lowercase Latin x remains intact.

- **ц**: Continuous 20-unit round terminal cap, with vertical tangent joins and bottom retained at -130. Approved body and upper flare retained.
- **щ**: Same rounded terminal as ц, translated 242 units; approved three-stem body retained.
- **х**: Cyrillic-only optical adaptation of native x: top 510 to 480, original terminal thickness preserved, central diagonals shortened and width reduced 3%; sidebearings opened. Original Latin x untouched.
- **ь**: Share approved ъ head, upper shoulder, right oval and counter; align top at 510 and bowl at 350/-9. Keep a curled lower-left return and no left foot. Shared 68-unit stem, 75-unit right stroke, 25-unit hairlines and advance 451.

Cyrillic х previously copied the exact native x outline. Its optical top is now
480 rather than 510. The upper and lower terminal zones keep their original
23-unit thickness; only the central diagonal span is shortened 30 units.
A 3% horizontal reduction brings the middle closer to the native stem weight.
Its advance changes from 385 to 393; measured sidebearings provide more air.
The ъх and хн body gaps are balanced with a small -5 correction on ъх.

ь uses the approved ъ right oval, shoulder, full counter and native head exactly.
Both have a 510-unit top, bowl from -9 to 350, a 68-unit stem, 75-unit right
stroke and 25-unit hairlines. The soft sign retains a curled lower-left return
and has no hard-sign left foot. Advance 426 to 451 matches the common bowl;
paired Cyrillic positioning follows the approved hard-sign counterpart.
The approved ъ and ф outlines and metrics are unchanged.

ц/щ keep the approved cups, stems and upper flared descender exits. At the last
part of the tail, the contour flows vertically into a true 20-unit round cap.
The bottom remains -130; cap quarters and the adjacent curves have matching
tangents. Their advances and stored left sidebearings remain unchanged.

Exactly four outlines change. Metrics change only for х/ь. 19 new Cyrillic
pair deltas preserve existing lookups and Latin shaping. 480 pairs
involving these four letters are sampled every 4 units from -205 to 740;
minimum scanline gap is 4.00. This is a sampled diagnostic, not an
exhaustive mathematical collision proof. See kern-corrections.json and proofs.

All other outlines and metrics, 285 Latin mappings per style and original
Italic TTF/WOFF2 bytes are preserved. Compilation, NFD accents, WOFF2 roundtrip,
adjacent-point and independent flattened crossing checks pass. The complete
source pipeline reproduces the final outlines, metrics and layout tables.
The eight-file download includes both unchanged original OFL licenses.
The original static portfolio thumbnail is preserved byte for byte.
