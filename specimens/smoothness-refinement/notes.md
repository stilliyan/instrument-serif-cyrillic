# Regular 0.570 — smoother в connection and calmer м peaks

The user approved smoothing the tight connection under the upper в loop and
removing its tiny triangular opening while retaining the preferred lower oval.
They also approved lowering the pointed tops of м slightly to improve the
rhythm in “Думите имат характер.” The proof uses actual 0.560 and 0.570 exports
at the same em and shared baselines, including 16, 24 and 32 pixel text.

- **м**: Translate the upper construction down 12 units; retain all lower coordinates, original width, advance and serif thickness. Control bound top 527 to 515; pointed ink peaks optically align with adjacent lowercase.
- **в**: Round the sharp outer waist with a small tangent-matched fillet and remove the triangular pinhole contour. Retain the preferred 0.510 lower oval, both counters, upper loop, width and advance.

Exactly two outlines change. All advance widths, sidebearings and layout tables
are unchanged. No new kerning corrections are required. 244 pairs
involving these letters were sampled every 4 units from -205 to 740. Minimum
sampled scanline gap is 2.00 units; the three inherited lower-serif gaps
in Ам, Км and мА remain unchanged. No reduced clearance is introduced. This is
a sampled diagnostic, not an exhaustive mathematical collision proof.

The lower в oval, both main counters, upper loop and metrics are checked for
exact equality outside the small outer fillet. The fourth accidental pinhole
contour is removed. The м lower construction stays point for point unchanged.
All other outlines and metrics, 285 Latin mappings per style and original
Italic TTF/WOFF2 bytes are preserved. Compilation, NFD accents, WOFF2 roundtrip,
adjacent-point and independent flattened crossing checks pass. The complete
source pipeline reproduces the final outlines, metrics and layout tables.
The eight-file download includes both unchanged original OFL licenses.
